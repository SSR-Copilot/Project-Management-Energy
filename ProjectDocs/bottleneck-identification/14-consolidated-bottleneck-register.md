## 5. Why there is no P0

P0 means severe degradation, failure or significant operational impact. Nothing in this audit meets it on evidence: no finding shows a user-blocking failure or a data-integrity defect in the current code (the three D165 P0s, CB-01, CB-02 and AD-01, are fixed and switched ON per the NET audit). The highest-impact items are P1 (10 raw, 9 unique) and are mostly a few seconds of avoidable latency on specific actions. That is a statement about the evidence available, which contains no production telemetry: if the Application Insights data (D161) shows a screen far slower than these derived figures, priorities should be revisited.
